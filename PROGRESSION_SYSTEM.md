# PROGRESSION SYSTEM — What Growth Looks Like

**Status:** Proposed. The user-facing layer over `MOTIVATION_SYSTEM.md` (policy) and `ASSESSMENT_FRAMEWORK.md` (measurement).
**The test every element must pass:** *does this make someone a better thinker?*
**Date:** 2026-07-25

---

## 1. The design problem

Users need to see growth. Reasoning growth is invisible, slow, and has no natural signal. Fill that vacuum with invented numbers and you get gamification that corrodes the motivation it was meant to serve; leave it empty and people cannot tell they are improving and leave.

The resolution runs through one principle:

> **Show them evidence, not points.**
>
> Every element below is either (a) a real measurement they can trust, (b) a real artifact they produced, or (c) a real first they achieved. Nothing is a number we invented to make a bar move.

Chess is the proof this works. Players return for decades with no XP, no confetti and no daily quest, because **Elo is honest** — it goes down, it cannot be farmed, and it means something. An honest number that can fall is more motivating than a generous one that only rises, because only the first one tells you something true about yourself.

## 2. The four layers

```
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ ① THE PROFILE      measured ability, with error bars  │  slow · honest
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ ② FIRSTS           the moment you first did a thing   │  fast · emotional
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ ③ TITLES           rare, earned, permanent            │  slow · social
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ ④ THE RECORD       everything you actually wrote      │  cumulative · proof
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Each solves a different problem: ① is trustworthy but slow, so ② carries the early weeks; ③ gives something displayable without a ranking; ④ is the artifact nobody can take away.

---

## 3. Layer ① — The Profile

Fully specified in `ASSESSMENT_FRAMEWORK.md` §3. Its progression role:

- **Never a single number.** A vector across strands, with confidence intervals.
- **Change is the headline.** *"Causal reasoning ↑31 since month 1"* beats any absolute value.
- **It can fall**, and that is what makes it worth watching. Current form alongside a permanent peak — the chess model, honest in both directions.
- **Private.** No user sees another's.
- **Reviewed seasonally**, not daily. A number you check every morning is a currency; one you meet every ninety days is a mirror.

**Progression's contribution here is restraint.** The temptation is to surface the profile constantly. Quarterly is right: reasoning does not change weekly, and a metric that visibly moves faster than the underlying reality is a lie that trains people to game it.

---

## 4. Layer ② — Firsts

The most underrated progression mechanic available, and nearly free.

**A First is the recorded moment you first did something that matters.** Not a badge for volume — a timestamped event in your own history.

| First | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| First position taken | You began |
| First argument published | You said why |
| First evidence attached | You supported it |
| **First self-catch** | You fixed it before the coach spoke — the hinge (`IDENTITY_DESIGN.md` §4) |
| **First mind changed** — theirs | Your reasoning moved a human being |
| **First mind changed** — yours | You updated under evidence |
| First steelman an opponent endorsed | You understood them better than they expected |
| First counterargument answered before it was raised | You anticipated |
| First evidence atom verified into the graph | You contributed to something permanent |
| First time your suggestion improved someone else's argument | You taught |
| First calibrated month | Your confidence matched reality |
| First minority position that later became the majority | You were early and right |

Firsts work because they are **emotionally dense, cost nothing to compute, and are impossible to farm** — you can only have a first once. They carry the first three months, which is exactly the window where measured progress is too slow to be visible and users are most likely to leave.

They are also the natural content of the monthly retrospective: *"a year ago this week, you changed your mind for the first time."*

---

## 5. Layer ③ — Titles

The **chess-title model**, not the badge model: few, rare, permanent, meaningful, and earned by behaviour rather than by score.

Chess titles work because there are only a handful, they take years, they never expire, and everyone knows roughly what they mean. Badge systems fail for the inverse reasons.

### Practice titles — from what you do

| Title | Earned by |
|---|---|
| **Evidence Builder** | 25 atoms verified into the graph |
| **Bridge-Builder** | 25 missing warrants supplied in others' arguments |
| **Steelmanner** | 10 steelmen endorsed by an opponent |
| **Counterargument Master** | 50 arguments pre-empting the strongest objection |
| **Persuasion Craftsman** | 25 persuasion events |
| **Curator** | A collection relied on by 50 people |
| **Mentor** | 25 suggestions adopted that measurably improved another's argument |

### Character titles — the rarest, and the ones that define us

| Title | Earned by |
|---|---|
| **Mind-Changer** | 10 position changes after engaging contrary evidence |
| **Corrector** | 10 public corrections of your own published arguments |
| **Calibrated** | Four consecutive seasons of near-diagonal calibration |

**The character titles must be the most prestigious things in the product.** Every other platform in existence rewards you for holding your ground; making these the pinnacle is the cheapest and most durable defence we have against becoming a debate club for smug people.

**Rules.** Displayed at the user's option, never ranked, never sorted, permanent once earned, and revocable only on detected abuse. There is no title for volume, for streaks, or for popularity.

## 6. Layer ④ — The Record

Everything you actually wrote, dated, with what you cited and how you changed.

This is the layer that does not feel like progression while you are earning it and becomes the most valuable thing you own after a year. It is the credential substrate (`ASSESSMENT_FRAMEWORK.md` §7), the retrospective engine, and the switching cost.

**The single most powerful progression surface in the entire product is not a chart.** It is your argument from a year ago, shown beside one from last week, with no commentary. Seeing your own improvement in your own words is more convincing than any number we could compute, and it costs us nothing but retrieval.

## 7. Levels — and why they are bands, not numbers

You asked for levels. Numbers invite ranking and Goodharting, so: **named bands per strand**, coarse enough to be honest.

```
 Apprentice → Practitioner → Adept → Craftsman → Master
```

Five bands, per strand, criterion-referenced against defined standards, awarded only with sufficient measurement confidence, and requiring **Arena evidence** for the top two — drills alone can never take you past Adept (`REASONING_SKILL_TREE.md` §5).

Bands beat numbers because they are honest about our resolution (we cannot distinguish 71 from 74), they cannot be summed into an overall rank, and they read as *stages of a craft* rather than *points scored* — which is the framing that supports mastery orientation rather than performance orientation.

A realistic user is **Craftsman in Structure, Practitioner in Evidence, Apprentice in Calibration**. That asymmetry is the point: it is a picture of a person, not a position in a queue.

## 8. Milestones

Long-horizon anchors between the fast Firsts and the slow Profile.

| Milestone | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **First season complete** | 90 days. You have a baseline |
| **The Turn** | The season your coach-intervention rate first halves |
| **First transfer** | Demonstrated the skill in a domain never practised |
| **Blind-verified improvement** | Human raters preferred your recent work over your early work, without knowing which was which |
| **Reasoning Portfolio** | Enough verified record to share externally |
| **Ten seasons** | Two and a half years of training |

**The Turn deserves emphasis.** The season when the coach halves its interventions is the clearest evidence that we are teaching rather than assisting, and it should be presented as the achievement it is:

> *"Season 4. I spoke half as often as last season. You're catching things I used to catch for you."*

A product celebrating its own reduced necessity is unusual, and it is the strongest possible signal that the progression system is measuring learning rather than usage.

## 9. What each element does for the thinking

The test you set, applied honestly to every mechanic:

| Element | Does it make someone a better thinker? |
|---|---|
| Profile (measured, quarterly) | **Yes** — accurate self-knowledge directs practice |
| Firsts | **Indirectly** — retention through the invisible-progress valley |
| Practice titles | **Yes** — each names a behaviour that *is* the skill |
| Character titles | **Strongly** — makes mind-changing high-status, which is the hardest behaviour to sustain |
| Bands | **Yes** — criterion-referenced, so they describe competence |
| The Record | **Strongly** — self-comparison is the most convincing evidence of growth |
| The Turn | **Strongly** — celebrates declining dependence |
| Streaks | **Weakly** — scaffolding only; humane form only |

Anything that could not fill the right-hand column honestly is not in this document.

## 10. What we are not building

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| A single overall level | Ranked within a week; Goodharted within two |
| Leaderboards | Performance orientation; the most toxic mechanic available |
| Volume XP | Overjustification; rewards quantity over quality |
| Daily quests / quotas | Controlling feedback; produces filler |
| Cosmetic unlocks | Rewards unrelated to the skill teach that the skill is not the reward |
| Loss-framed streak pressure | Coercion dressed as care |
| Public faculty scores | Punishes beginners into silence |
| Titles for popularity | Popularity is never the objective |

Every one of these would raise engagement. That is exactly why the list exists — **if a metric review ever proposes one, the correct response is to question the metric.**

---

## 11. Two checks that could tell us this is wrong

**The hidden-progression holdout.** A cohort sees no profile, no titles, no bands. If they write *more* and *better*, we have built a currency rather than a mirror and should delete it (`MOTIVATION_SYSTEM.md` §12).

**The gaming audit.** Each season, take the top decile by progression and hand-inspect their work. If the fastest progressors are not visibly the best reasoners, the system is measuring the wrong thing — and we should fix the measure before we ship another mechanic on top of it.
